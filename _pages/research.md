## Research

My *current* research spans three areas:

- **Collective Intelligence and Privacy-preserving Data Sharing**: *Federated Learning*, where organizations (e.g., hospitals) jointly train AI models while sharing only model parameters, not raw data; and, *Federated Unlearning*, which removes specific organizational data's influence from a trained model to uphold the right to be forgotten.

- **Network economics**: Incentive mechanisms and game-theoretic analysis that reward *cooperative* and *competitive* organizations in proportion to their contributions (e.g., data volume, gains in global model performance).

- **Networked systems**: Blockchain for auditable, secure data sharing, and security for next-generation (5G and beyond) networks.

My *past* research interests include: 

- Wireless Sensor Networks

- Cloud computing 

- Deep Learning-based IoT systems

- Software-Defined Networking
